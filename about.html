What We Do

Built for the F&B Trade

Harmony International Food is a Singapore-registered B2B food supply business that sources and distributes fresh and frozen protein products to restaurants, hot pot operators, commercial kitchens, and food service companies across the island.

We act as a direct supply channel — bridging international and local producers with Singapore's F&B businesses. This keeps our pricing lean and our supply chain tight, with a single point of contact for ordering, quotation, and delivery coordination.

With over 150 SKUs across five product categories — from fresh pork cuts and seafood to processed hotpot items — we offer the breadth of range that busy kitchens need without the complexity of managing multiple suppliers.
